# ChaiSniffer Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-31  
**Languages:** [简体中文](PRIVACY.md) | [繁體中文](PRIVACY.zh-TW.md) | [English](PRIVACY.en.md)

## Summary

ChaiSniffer **does not collect, upload, or sell** your personal data. Sniffing, downloads, and playback run **on your device**.

---

## Extension data

| Data | Use | Leaves device? |
|------|-----|----------------|
| Media URLs on the current page | Resource list | **No** |
| Cookie / Referer / User-Agent | Local download/playback headers | **No** |
| Settings | `chrome.storage` / local helper config | **No** |

No analytics SDK or ad tracking is built in.

---

## Helper app data

The helper app runs locally to call ffmpeg, write downloads, and launch players. It does not phone home; network requests go to media CDNs you access.
